Nageshwar Jyotirlinga

Location

Nageshwar is located in Dwarka, Gujarat, near the Arabian Sea. The temple is situated close to the famous Dwarkadhish Temple and is part of the sacred Dwarka pilgrimage circuit.

History

The Nageshwar Temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva as the Lord of Serpents. According to Hindu mythology, the temple was established to commemorate the salvation of a demon named Daruk by Lord Shiva. The demon was killed at this location, and Lord Shiva manifested here as the Nageshwar Jyotirlinga. The temple is located in Dwarka, one of the most sacred pilgrimage destinations in Hinduism, also known as the kingdom of Lord Krishna. The temple has been an important center of worship for centuries and is mentioned in various Hindu texts and Puranas. The architecture of the temple reflects ancient Hindu temple construction techniques with intricate stone carvings and ornate decorations. The temple is surrounded by the Arabian Sea, adding to its scenic beauty and spiritual significance. Dwarka is believed to be the ancient capital of Lord Krishna's kingdom and is home to many important temples, making the entire region a major pilgrimage destination.

Significance

Nageshwar is the tenth Jyotirlinga and is unique as it is the only Jyotirlinga located in western Gujarat near the sea. The temple holds special significance for those seeking protection and relief from fear and anxiety, as Lord Shiva is invoked here as the Lord who liberates from all fears.
